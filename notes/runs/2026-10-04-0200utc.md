### The 10-03 decomposition was wrong — the overlap window ended one day too late

The split must decompose `new(7d to T1) - old(7d to T0)` where `T1 = T0 + 1d`. The windows are
`[T0-7d, T0]` and `[T0-6d, T0+1d]`, so **the shared overlap is `[T0-6d, T0]` — a 6-day window ending at the
OLD build's timestamp, not the new one.**

The 10-03 run used the 6-day window ending at **its own** stamp (Oct 3) and published overlap **+1.3143pt**,
roll-off **+2.5076pt**, new information **+0.2340pt**, "roll-off 10.7x new info".

Recomputed first-hand with the correct overlap (6d to **Oct 2**, = **+1.5546pt**):
