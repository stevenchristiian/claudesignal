The 10-13 run's check 9 caught *all three firmed by two to three points* as a real error (only two
routes had a prior reading) and recorded it as **corrected**. It **was** corrected in the BTC
summary — and the **published build still carried the error in a catalyst chip**:
`And all three firmed — Each moved up two to three points over the day`. It went live and sat on the
page for a day.

> **A CORRECTION IS NOT APPLIED UNTIL EVERY STRING CARRYING THE CLAIM IS CHANGED.** Check 9 reads each
> published string **independently**, which is what lets it catch things — and is exactly why a fix
> found in one string must be **grepped for across all of them** before the check is called done.
> **Standing addition to check 9: after each correction, grep the whole build for the corrected claim,
> not only the string it was found in.** This is §7's coverage rule (*a verdict without a coverage
> figure is not a result*) applied to the *remedy* rather than to the test. Today's run applied each of
> its 17 corrections through an anchor-count assertion that **fails if the anchor does not appear
> exactly once**, which would have caught this by construction.
