## A SECOND ASSERTION FAULT, AND THE NOTES CAUGHT IT: the Treasury off-by-one

The first Treasury read of this run disagreed with these notes on **every October date** — Oct 9 at
**2Y 4.47% / 10Y 5.13%** against the published **4.80% / 5.24%** — and the pattern was not a uniform
shift (2Y and 10Y lower, 30Y *higher*), which is the signature of a **column mapping error**, not a

**Cause: an awk off-by-one.** The header is
`Date,"1 Mo","1.5 Month","2 Mo","3 Mo","4 Mo","6 Mo","1 Yr","2 Yr",…`, so **`$9` is the 2-year, not
`$8`**, and **`$13` is the 10-year, not `$12`**. With correct indices every figure in these notes
reconciles **exactly** (09/29 4.89/5.26/5.59, 09/30 4.88/5.29/5.64, 10/01 4.78/5.24/5.61,
10/02 4.83/5.28/5.63, 10/09 4.80/5.24/5.60 with 2s10s 44bp).

**This file diagnosed its own reader.** §8's 10-05 entry records that **`5.17` is the SEVEN-YEAR on the
10/02 row** — and the wrong extraction returned exactly 5.17 for 10/02's "10-year". A stored near-miss
from eight runs ago identified the defect in one step.
> **A SYSTEMATIC DISAGREEMENT WITH A VERIFIED BASELINE IS A BUG IN THE READER BEFORE IT IS NEWS.** The
