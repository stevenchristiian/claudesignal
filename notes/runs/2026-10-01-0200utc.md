## XRP — the trigger did not fire, and the reason is a wrong date

The branch: a second cloture vote **passing** adds 0.2; the motion **failing**, or the **window closing
unused**, cuts 0.1. On 2026-09-22 the undated "window closing unused" was given a date — **Oct 1** — on the
explicit ground that *the Senate is due to leave Oct 1*. The 09-30 build stated in advance that the cut
would fire on this run.

**It does not, because the premise is false.** Read first-hand from `senate.gov/legislative/2026_schedule.htm`
(Tentative 2026 Legislative Schedule, 119th Congress 2nd Session, updated Nov 21 2025), which says in its own
words that *the list below identifies expected non-legislative periods (days that the Senate will not be in
session)*:

> **Oct 05 - Nov 06  State Work Period**  (Columbus Day - Oct 12)

Oct 1 is a Thursday and Oct 4 a Sunday. The Senate's **last scheduled legislative day before the recess is
Friday Oct 2**. Secondary reporting on the published calendar agrees: Roll Call / Bloomberg Government list
the non-legislative weeks as those beginning Oct 4, Oct 11, Oct 18, Oct 25 and Nov 1.

**So the window is open for two more days and firing today would fire a window-closed condition while the
window is open.** That is the same error the Hormuz entry already records in the other direction — a branch
that names a condition means that condition. Corrected: **the 0.1 cut fires on the Oct 5 run**, absent a
second cloture vote on Oct 1 or Oct 2. No amendment to the branch; only the date it was anchored to.

### The near-miss that would have reversed this
A tracker page summarised the same block as *the Senate's next **work period** runs from October 5 to
November 6* — which reads as a session and would have confirmed the Oct 1 departure. On the Senate's own
calendar a **State Work Period is the opposite of a session**: it is time in the home state, and the page
says so explicitly. The same four words mean the reverse of each other depending on who writes them.

### Status unchanged
Cloture failed **49-50** on Sept 15 over ethics drafting, not the whip count; Tillis motion to reconsider
preserved. A CLARITY Act tracker **last updated Sept 30** records no second cloture vote and no scheduled
floor action after Sept 15. CR signed Sept 2 funds to **Dec 11**, so no shutdown competes for floor time.
