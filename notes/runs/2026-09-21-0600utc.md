Two assertions fired on genuine defects, which is the checklist working:

- **Check 7** caught that Python's `re.subn` with a `lambda` replacement does **not** expand
  backreferences — all three as-of labels had been written with a literal `\g<1>` in them. Rebuilt
  with the replacement passed as a string.
- **Check 9** caught a false superlative: XRP was described as having "the oldest such high of the
  six" when **BTC's twenty-one-day high (Sept 3) is older** than XRP's (Sept 14). Also removed a
  self-contradiction where XRP was called the largest gainer one clause before the deck declined to
  publish an ordering claim. Replaced with the figure plus an explicit refusal to rank, and a
  verified margin (furthest below its own window highs, by 1.64pt over Solana).

**Check 10 assertion audited rather than obeyed.** It reported 4 "page errors"; all were HTTP 429
from `api.coingecko.com`, the page's live-price source, and they appear identically on the *old*
published file. Re-scoped to distinguish uncaught JS exceptions (0) from external resource errors.
Note `#assetGrid` renders 0 on any version of the file when CoinGecko 429s, since the cards are
built from live data — an API-availability signal, not a broken build.

Also fixed before validating: Playwright's Chromium trusted no HTTPS at all
(`ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID`), which would have made the render unfaithful and silently broken the
page's own live fetches. Resolved with the supported Chrome `CACertificates` enterprise policy
carrying the agent-proxy CA. TLS verification was never disabled.
